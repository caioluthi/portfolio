Data product case study

Flagging consignment contracts that pace below target, before they expire.

Second Avenue only found out a contract was losing money after it expired. I sized the problem, specified a data product, tested the bet with a prototype, let the results change the plan, and planned the build with Engineering.

Second Avenue is a fictional consignment retailer; the use case comes from a problem I worked on professionally. People and figures are simulated.
