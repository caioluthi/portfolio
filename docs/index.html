Data product case study

Flagging consignment contracts that pace below target, before they expire.

An end-to-end data product, from discovery to a working build, for Second Avenue, a fictional consignment retailer. The use case comes from a problem I worked on professionally; the company, people and data here are simulated.

The short version

Problem
Second Avenue guarantees consignors a target price and pays the shortfall when a contract misses. About 15% of contracts miss, costing roughly $3.6M a year, and nobody sees it coming until the contract expires.
Product
A governed pacing metric and daily risk flag across contracts, listings and sales, with natural-language questions on top.
Success
Flag underperformers a median of 30+ days before expiry, without more than 40% false alarms.
